Introduction

This portfolio presents my best outputs from the Operating Systems course, including module assessments and laboratory activities. The course taught me how an operating system manages processes, memory, files, and hardware to keep a computer running smoothly. Through each output, I show the challenges I faced, my approach, and what I learned. This portfolio also serves as a reminder that Computer Science is a field worth exploring further, because understanding how systems work gives us the power to build and improve the technology of the future.
